## Project Overview
**EVA (Electric Vehicle Assistant)** - An AI chatbot designed to assist FPL customer service agents with troubleshooting issues related to EV chargers.

- **Objective**: Enhance FPL customer service agent efficiency for EV charger support
- **Target Users**: FPL customer service agents
- **Key Features**: Real-time troubleshooting guidance, customer account integration, billing support
- **Performance Goal**: <2 second response time
