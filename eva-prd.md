# EVA - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Introduction

### Overview
This document outlines the requirements for EVA, an AI chatbot designed to assist FPL customer service agents with troubleshooting issues related to EV chargers.

### Background
As FPL seeks to improve customer support efficiency, EVA will empower agents with quick access to solutions and troubleshooting guidance, enhancing their ability to resolve customer issues promptly.

## 2. Objectives

- To enhance the efficiency of FPL customer service agents by providing quick access to troubleshooting information and solutions.
- To reduce the time taken to resolve customer issues, leading to improved customer satisfaction.
- To prepare for a future phase where EVA will be launched directly to customers.

## 3. Scope

### Included:
- **Real-time support** for common issues faced by customers.
- **Guidance for troubleshooting** firmware issues, welding issues, and leakage error codes.
- **Assistance in addressing** common billing and Wi-Fi connectivity questions.

### Excluded:
- Direct customer-facing support.
- Integration with customer management systems.

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary Users
FPL customer service agents handling EV charger-related inquiries.

### Demographics
Internal staff with varying levels of technical expertise.

## 5. Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- **EVA will require agent to input customer account number** for home charging issues so that it can access all relevant customer information (EV model, charger type, call history, etc.).
- **EVA should provide step-by-step guidance** for troubleshooting common issues such as chargers not working, firmware updates, billing error codes.
- **The chatbot must offer solutions** to common billing questions related to Wi-Fi connectivity and charger billing.
- **Integration with the FPL support system** to log queries and track resolutions.

### Non-functional Requirements

- **Fast response with solutions** delivered in under 2 seconds.
